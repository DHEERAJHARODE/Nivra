Our History

A Trusted Wholesale Handloom Partner Since 1978

Threads woven through generations of mastery

Established in 1978, Nivrá by Porwals carries forward a family tradition deep in Indian textile craft. We stand at the intersection of timeless handloom heritage and the rigorous formatting demands of global commercial scale interior projects.

Today, we serve as a trusted B2B sourcing matrix for interior designers, hospitality labels, and major retail networks across Indore and cross-India states. Every order carries the fingerprint of its maker — a guarantee of authentic structural durability.
