Our History

A Trusted Wholesale Handloom Partner Since 1978

Threads woven through generations of mastery

In the 1950s, three brothers, Ramchandra, Srinivas and Madanlal, left home for Indore and sold dhurries and bedsheets from a footpath. Built on trust and quality, they grew and in 1978, Porwal Handlooms was born.

Nivrá, from Srinivas and Ram, means calm retreat in Sanskrit and honours all three. Today we carry that quality to buyers across India.
