Our Legacy

Threads woven through generations of mastery

Since 1978, Nivra has stood at the intersection of timeless Indian textile heritage and the evolving demands of global interiors.

Today, we are a trusted B2B sourcing partner for interior designers, hospitality brands, and retail chains across indore. Every piece carries the fingerprint of its maker a guarantee of authenticity you cannot find in machine-made alternatives.
